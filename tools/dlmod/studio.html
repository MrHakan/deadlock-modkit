Images

The hero's card on the hero select screen and in the HUD, portraits, minimap icon, background and ability icons. Drop a PNG or JPEG on one: it is fitted to the game's size, cropped in the middle.

Text

Ability names and descriptions, role, playstyle and lore, as the game shows them. Keep the {s:...} and <span> parts: the game fills in numbers and colours there.

5 · Rig

dlmod copies bone weights from the hero's own body. If your model stands in another pose (arms down, T-pose), it bends the hero to match first.

Advanced rig

Quick bends:
The hero's own skeleton bends here and moves your model by its weights, as the game does. The clips are the hero's own. Melee, reloads and casts play in game over the movement through a bone mask, which is picked for them here; the game blends more layers than this, so treat it as close, not exact. The quick bends are made up.
