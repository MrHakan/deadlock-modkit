# v0.3.2 · The shop panel is readable again

Experimental HUD for Deadlock client build **6864**.

- **In the shop, the panel showed as a thin dark strip down the left edge.** v0.3.1 limited the panel's width with a percentage (`max-width: 21%`), and in game that squeezed it to a few pixels. The panel is 354 pixels wide again, under Quick Buy on the left side of the shop.

Everything else is as in v0.3.1. In the hideout, where there is no enemy team, the panel shows "Waiting for enemy lineup."; suggestions appear in a match. When the script loads, the console shows `[DCI] Counter items HUD loaded`.

Close Deadlock first, then press `u` in dlmod to update the HUD.

All 17 JavaScript, 9 Python and 21 Go tests passed, and the mod was built against the live build-6864 stock HUD.
