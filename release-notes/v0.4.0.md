# v0.4.0 · Model studio: drop a 3D model on a hero

Experimental HUD for Deadlock client build **6864** (unchanged since v0.3.5), and a new part of dlmod for custom character models.

### dlmod: custom models

- **Drop a `.glb`, `.gltf` or `.obj` file on `dlmod.exe`, or run `dlmod studio`.** The model studio opens in your browser (it runs on your computer only):
  1. Pick a hero.
  2. Drop the model with its textures, or a `.zip` of them, and see it over the hero.
  3. Adjust size, turn and position.
  4. Choose which hero parts stay (the gun by default).
  5. Rig it, and bend it to check the weights.
  6. Install it as the mod *Custom model: \<hero\>*.

  `dlmod model <hero> <file>` does the same without the browser.
- **The hero keeps everything but its looks:** skeleton, animations, hitboxes, physics and abilities. dlmod rewrites the hero's `.vmdl_c` around the new mesh and copies every other block unchanged. No Valve tools, Blender or Source 2 Viewer are needed.
- **Automatic rigging.**
  - A rigged model keeps its own weights, with its joints mapped onto the hero's bones by name or by position. Weapons follow the joint they hang from.
  - A model without a rig copies weights from the hero's own body. Clothes, hair and capes are left out of that copy.
  - When the model stands in another pose (arms down, T-pose), the hero is bent to match first.
  - Weights are smoothed across the surface, and props held at the hip follow the hand.
  - Rigging 50,000 vertices takes about 2 seconds.
- **Materials and textures.** Each material becomes one for Deadlock's hero shader. Its PNG or JPEG colour texture is compressed to BC1 or BC3.
- **Formats read:**
  - Binary KeyValues3 versions 4 and 5.
  - meshoptimizer-compressed mesh buffers, ported from ValveResourceFormat and checked against meshoptimizer's own test vectors.
  - glTF skins and `EXT_meshopt_compression`.
  - OBJ with MTL.

**Not yet tested in game.** Please report whether the model shows up and animates. Known limits:
- WebP textures and FBX files are not read: export from Blender as `.glb` with PNG textures.
- If the game's own models switched to a newer compression, dlmod says so instead of building a broken mod.
- The 3D preview loads three.js from cdn.jsdelivr.net.

Everything else is as in v0.3.5.

All Go, JavaScript and Python tests passed, including one that puts a model on a sample hero through the studio's own web API and installs it.
